## Note on contributor breadth

For context when reading the Lifecycle Policy's expectation of *"a healthy number of
committers from at least two organizations"*: commit authorship to date spans OpenAI (14
commits), UiPath, Microsoft, Warp, GitHub, JetBrains and several individual contributors, so
the contributor base is already multi-organisation. The gap is in **who can merge**, not in
who shows up.
